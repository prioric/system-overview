Market
Seven water boards spend about €1.1 billion a year on pipe renewal, and Westland now tenders large lots.
Westland spends about a third of the boards' total. Its program for 2027 to 2037 sets lots of €60 million and more. The first three lots open for bids in June 2027.
Water boards' renewal programs, June 2026; Westland tender notice, October 2026
Customers
Westland paid 46% of Alder's revenue in 2025, up from 21% in 2021.
The six smaller water boards paid 41% and the two gas network operators 13%. Each board uses the pipes and pays for the work itself. Every award sits on the public tender record.
Alder annual reports 2021 and 2025
Competitors
Alder is the smallest of the four builders that win most of the boards' pipe work.
Hollis Build had revenue of €920 million in 2025, against Alder's €84m. All four compete for the same lots and the same licensed welders. The floor is a board that delays renewal and repairs old pipe with its own crews.
Competitors' annual reports 2025; national welding register 2026
Assets
Alder holds twenty years of acceptance records at every water board.
The records let Alder pass the past-performance gate in any tender. It held four open Westland contracts worth €61 million at the end of 2025. Its cash was €7.6 million.
Alder annual report 2025
Capabilities
Alder delivers lots under €40 million with its own crews, again and again.
It won 27 lots from 2021 to 2025, every one under €40 million. The smaller boards recorded fewer defects on its sections than their average. It has never led a lot above €38 million.
Boards' award lists and acceptance reports, 2021 to 2025
Limits
Each lot needs a bank guarantee of 10% of its value, and Alder's largest so far was €3.8 million.
A €60 million Westland lot needs a €6 million guarantee. Receivables rose from 19% of revenue in 2021 to 31% in 2025, while cash stayed at 9%. The board approves every contract over €5 million.
Westland general conditions, 2024; Alder annual report 2025

Where the facts pull against each other

1The guarantee outgrows Alder

Westland's new lots start at €60 million and each needs a €6 million guarantee, while the largest guarantee Alder has posted was €3.8 million.

Who pays: Alder's owners and staff, if Alder cannot bid on Westland's lots when they open in June 2027.

2One buyer, more unpaid work

Westland paid 46% of Alder's revenue in 2025, while receivables rose to 31% of revenue and cash stayed at 9%.

Who pays: Alder, which carries the unpaid work with its own cash and pays its bank a fee on every guarantee.

3The same welders

Alder grew its staff from 330 to 410, most of them welders, from the same two schools that its larger competitors hire from.

Who pays: Alder, in wages, if Hollis Build or Carrow Group hire more welders for the 2027 lots. [INFERRED]

The decision

Whether Alder bids for Westland's first €60 million lots, and how it covers the €6 million guarantee each one needs.

Who decides: Alder's board of directors. By when: June 2027, when the first lots open for bids.

  • Bid in a consortium with a larger builder Cost: a share of each lot's margin, to a partner that also competes for Alder's welders. Who pays: Alder's owners.
  • Ask Kestrel Bank for a €6 million guarantee Cost: a yearly fee for as long as the lot runs, and Alder Holding's approval to put cash at risk beyond one year. Who pays: Alder Holding.
  • Keep to lots under €40 million at the smaller boards Cost: the Westland work that paid 46% of Alder's revenue in 2025. Who pays: Alder's staff and owners.

If nobody decides: Alder's bid list for 2026 holds no lot over €40 million, so the first Westland lots go to the larger builders when the award list comes out in December 2027.
