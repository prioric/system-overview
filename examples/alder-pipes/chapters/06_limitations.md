# Alder Pipes · Limitations

## Summary
Two things bind Alder: the bank guarantee and its cash. The largest guarantee Alder has posted was €3.8 million, on its €38 million contract. Its receivables, the work done and invoiced but not yet paid, rose from 19% of revenue in 2021 to 31% in 2025. Its cash stayed at about 9% of revenue in the same years. So Alder carries more unpaid work every year with the same cash.

## Evidence
- A board asks for a bank guarantee of 10% of each lot's value, held for the whole contract. [Westland general conditions, January 2024, p. 4]
- The largest guarantee Alder has posted was €3.8 million, on its €38 million contract. [Alder annual report 2025, p. 18, April 2026]
- Receivables were 19% of revenue at the end of 2021. [Alder annual report 2021, p. 38, April 2022]
- Receivables were 31% of revenue at the end of 2025, or €26 million. [Alder annual report 2025, p. 41, April 2026]
- Cash was 9% of revenue at the end of 2025. [Alder annual report 2025, p. 40, April 2026]
- Kestrel Bank, Alder's only bank, charges a yearly fee on each guarantee for as long as the lot runs. [Alder annual report 2025, p. 42, April 2026]

### Jumps
- A lot of €60 million needs a guarantee of €6 million, above any guarantee Alder has posted. [Westland tender notice, 3 October 2026]

## Confidence
High. The figures come from the filed accounts and the board's general conditions.

## Unknowns
