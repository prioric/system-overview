# Alder Pipes · Competition

## Summary
Four builders win most of the boards' pipe work. Hollis Build is the largest and Alder Pipes is the smallest of the four. All four compete for the same lots and for the same licensed welders. Birch Civil, the competitor closest to Alder in size, keeps its largest buyer at 18% of its revenue. The floor is a board that delays renewal and repairs old pipe instead.

## Evidence
- Hollis Build had revenue of €920 million in 2025. [Hollis Build annual report 2025, p. 4, March 2026]
- Birch Civil's largest buyer paid 18% of its revenue in 2025. [Birch Civil annual report 2025, p. 9, March 2026]
- Hollis Build won the Eastbank lot in 2025 with a price below Alder's bid. [Westland award list, November 2025]
- Hollis Build and Carrow Group hire licensed welders from the same two training schools as Alder. [national welding register, 2026]
- The floor: a board can postpone renewal and pay its own crews for repairs, and two smaller boards did so in 2023. [smaller boards' annual reports 2023]
- A consortium of builders may bid on a lot as one, and each partner then posts the guarantee for its own share. [Westland general conditions, January 2024, p. 5]
