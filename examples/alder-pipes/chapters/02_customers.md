# Alder Pipes · Customers

## Summary
Alder's buyers are seven regional water boards and two gas network operators. The body that uses the pipes and the body that pays for the work are the same board. Each board buys through public tenders, so every choice it makes is on the public record. Westland paid 46% of Alder's revenue in 2025, up from 21% in 2021. The six smaller boards have awarded Alder work for twenty years.

## Evidence
- Westland paid 21% of Alder's revenue in 2021. [Alder annual report 2021, p. 20, April 2022]
- Westland paid 46% of Alder's revenue in 2025. [Alder annual report 2025, p. 22, April 2026]
- Alder won about a third of the lots it bid for at the six smaller boards between 2021 and 2025. [boards' tender award lists 2021 to 2025, published 2026]
- The smaller boards' acceptance reports name Alder's own crews and their low defect counts. [smaller boards' acceptance reports 2021 to 2025]
- A board chooses the lowest price that comes with a bank guarantee, once the builder passes a past-performance gate. [Westland general conditions, January 2024, p. 4]
- The boards are institutional buyers, so they leave no reviews; the tender award lists show what they actually chose. [boards' tender award lists 2021 to 2025, published 2026]
