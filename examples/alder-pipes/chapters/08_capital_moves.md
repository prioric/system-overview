# Alder Pipes · Capital moves

## Summary
Alder put its money into a second welding crew in 2025. It made no sales of units, closures or layoffs from 2021 to 2025. It hired welders every year. Its bid list for 2026 holds no lot over €40 million. In 2025 it won two Westland lots and finished one.

## Evidence
- **Invests.** Alder spent €3 million on a second welding crew in 2025, paid from cash. [Alder annual report 2025, p. 19, April 2026]
- **Invests.** Alder won two Westland lots in 2025, worth €19 million together, and finished one worth €9 million. [Alder annual report 2025, p. 19, April 2026]
- **Cuts.** The annual reports for 2021 to 2025 record no sale of a unit, no closure and no layoff. [Alder annual reports 2021 to 2025, April 2022 to April 2026]
- **Hires.** Alder's staff rose from 330 at the end of 2021 to 410 at the end of 2025, most of them welders. [Alder annual report 2025, p. 44, April 2026]
- Alder's bid list for 2026 holds no lot over €40 million. [Alder annual report 2025, p. 20, April 2026]

## Confidence
High. All of it comes from the filed annual reports.
