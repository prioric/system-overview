# Alder Pipes · Operating model

## Summary
The Alder family owns the company through a holding company. A board of directors runs it and approves the bid list and every contract over €5 million. Alder's bid manager is paid a bonus for each lot won, with no weight on the lot's size. Site managers are paid on sections accepted on time. Decisions on bids are made once a quarter, at the board meeting.

## Evidence
- Alder Holding, owned by the Alder family, holds all the shares. [Alder annual report 2025, p. 2, April 2026]
- The board of directors approves the bid list and every contract over €5 million. [Alder articles of association, filed 2019]
- The bid manager is paid a bonus for each lot won, with no weight on lot size. [Alder annual report 2025, p. 44, April 2026]
- Site managers are paid a bonus on sections accepted on time. [Alder annual report 2025, p. 44, April 2026]
- The board meets once a quarter, and the bid list is set at those meetings. [Alder annual report 2025, p. 45, April 2026]

### Who decides
- The board of directors: the bid list, and every contract over €5 million. [Alder articles of association, filed 2019]
- Alder Holding: any guarantee that puts the company's cash at risk beyond one year. [Alder articles of association, filed 2019]
- Kestrel Bank: the size and fee of every guarantee Alder posts. [Alder annual report 2025, p. 42, April 2026]
- Westland's tender board: which bid wins each Westland lot. [Westland general conditions, January 2024, p. 3]

## Confidence
High. The decision rights come from the filed articles, and the pay rules from the annual report.
