# Alder Pipes · Assets

## Summary
Alder owns a yard with its plant in the middle of the country. It holds twenty years of acceptance records at every water board, which let it pass the past-performance gate in any tender. At the end of 2025 it held four open Westland contracts worth €61 million together. The largest contract it has ever completed was worth €38 million. Its cash at the end of 2025 was €7.6 million.

## Evidence
- Alder owns its yard and plant, which the accounts carry at €11 million. It is likely to last for years, because the yard serves every board from one place. [Alder annual report 2025, p. 30, April 2026]
- Alder holds acceptance records at all seven water boards going back twenty years. They are likely to last, because the records only grow, but the tender formula counts them only as a pass at the gate. [Alder annual report 2025, p. 16, April 2026]
- Alder held four open Westland contracts at the end of 2025, worth €61 million together: €24 million, €18 million, €12 million and €7 million. They finish between 2027 and 2029. [Alder annual report 2025, p. 18, April 2026]
- The largest contract Alder has completed was worth €38 million, finished in 2024. [Alder annual report 2025, p. 18, April 2026]
- Cash at the end of 2025 was €7.6 million, 9% of revenue. [Alder annual report 2025, p. 40, April 2026]

High. Every figure comes from the filed annual report.
