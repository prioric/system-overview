# Alder Pipes · Context

## Summary
Alder Pipes lays water and gas pipelines for public utilities in one country. Seven regional water boards own the water pipes and renew them on programs they publish years ahead. Together the boards spend about €1.1 billion a year on renewal, and that figure has moved little since 2021. Westland is the largest board and spends about a third of the total. In October 2026 Westland published a program that bundles its work into much larger lots.

## Evidence
- The seven water boards spend about €1.1 billion a year on pipe renewal, against €1.0 billion in 2021. [water boards' published renewal programs, June 2026]
- Westland spends about a third of the boards' total renewal budget. [Westland renewal program 2027 to 2037, 3 October 2026]
- Two gas network operators tender pipe work separately, in lots far smaller than the water lots. [gas operators' tender calendars, 2026]
- Every board awards renewal work by public tender, one lot at a time, under the national public procurement law. [Westland general conditions, January 2024, p. 2]
- Westland's program for 2027 to 2037 bundles its work into lots of €60 million and more. [Westland tender notice, 3 October 2026]
- The first three lots of the Westland program open for bids in June 2027, and the award list is due in December 2027. [Westland tender notice, 3 October 2026]
- Alder Pipes is unlisted, so no stock market value exists to set beside its competitors. [Alder annual report 2025, p. 1, April 2026]

## Confidence
High. The spending figures come from the boards' own published programs, and the lot sizes from Westland's tender notice.

## Open questions
- Will the six smaller boards follow Westland and bundle their lots?

## Unknowns
- No public source gives the gas operators' total yearly spend.
