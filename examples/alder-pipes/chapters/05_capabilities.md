# Alder Pipes · Capabilities

## Summary
Alder employs its own welders and pipe layers and does not subcontract them. The smaller boards accept its pipe sections with fewer defects than their average, and have done so for five years. Alder bids for and delivers lots under €40 million again and again. It has never led a lot above €38 million.

## Evidence
- Alder employs its welders and pipe layers directly, 410 people at the end of 2025. [Alder annual report 2025, p. 44, April 2026]
- The six smaller boards recorded fewer defects on Alder's sections than their average in each year from 2021 to 2025. This is likely to last while the same crews stay, because the skill sits in the people. [smaller boards' acceptance reports 2021 to 2025]
- Alder won 27 lots between 2021 and 2025, every one under €40 million. [boards' tender award lists 2021 to 2025, published 2026]
- Alder has never led a consortium or a lot above €38 million. [Alder annual report 2025, p. 18, April 2026]

## Confidence
Medium. The defect record rests on the boards' reports, which do not publish their method.
